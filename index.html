The challenge (M1)

How might IE University become the most trusted platform for developing, proving and renewing professional capabilities by 2035, so that learners and employers choose IE for measurable career outcomes, not merely for a degree?

The 2035 future

Three drivers decide where IE must win

For each starred driver we imagined two futures and checked today's signals. Toggle the futures; the green tag marks the one our scenario uses.

Our scenario · Learning for Life, Proven in Practice
The year is 2035. Every learner has a cheap, highly personal AI tutor, so passing on knowledge is no longer a university's edge. Employers look for proof of applied judgment, teamwork and the ability to work with AI, and some first career steps have become less predictable. Students choose schools that offer credible assessment, real company projects, human networks and access to employers. Professionals come back for targeted learning throughout their careers.

Opportunities for IE

  • Distinctive employer partnerships
  • Verified portfolios and assessments
  • Premium peer networks
  • Lifelong alumni relationships and corporate reskilling

Threats to IE

  • Teaching content becomes a commodity
  • Pressure on premium tuition
  • A weaker first-job promise
  • New certifiers and corporate academies

IESE Business School

  • Revenue €116.1M (FY2022, latest published)
  • Technology €8.0M, research €6.8M

What we takeOur closest Spanish peer invests far less in new value. Our 5% is a deliberate step up.

Reference 3 · USA

Coursera

  • Revenue $757.5M (2025)
